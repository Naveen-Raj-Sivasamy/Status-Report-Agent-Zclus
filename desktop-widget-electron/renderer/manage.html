Stops the Weekly_Connect tab (and its landing-screen category) from auto-recreating itself. Turn this on FIRST, save, THEN delete the tab and its rows on Categories — deleting it before this is on just gets it recreated on the next tab-list refresh.

Rename a Tab

Renames the sheet tab itself AND rewrites every place that references its old name (Categories, Field Types, Report Configs, and Report/Hidden tab lists) — this is why renaming here is safer than renaming directly in Sheets, which only changes the tab and leaves everything else pointing at a name that no longer exists.

Report Configs

As many independent reports as you need — each with its own tabs, its own selected columns per tab, its own recipients, and (optionally) its own auto-send day/time. An org with just the one report only ever sees the one card below; add another any time a different team/category needs its own report with a different audience.

Connect Groups

Each group posts its Weekly Connect tickets to its own Teams channel — pick which group a ticket belongs to right on the ticket form. Add another group any time; no code change needed.
